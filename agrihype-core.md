# 🌾 Agrihype — Product Feature Specification

AI-Powered Agricultural Export Intelligence & Coordination Platform

---

## 🟢 Stage 1 — Validation MVP (Fundable Product)

### 🎯 Goal

Validate real-world usage by exporters and producers while proving market demand.

---

## 1. Authentication & Multi-Role Access

- Secure authentication (NextAuth)
- Organization-based accounts (multi-tenant ready)
- Role-based dashboards:
    - Admin
    - Exporter
    - Producer
    - Buyer
- Team member invitations
- Permission-scoped data access

---

## 2. Export Intelligence Dashboard (Core Value)

Help exporters decide:

- What to export
- Where to export
- When to export

Features:

- Country demand insights
- Seasonal export calendar
- Commodity demand heatmaps
- Price trend visualization
- Market summaries powered by AI

---

## 3. Export Readiness Scoring Engine

Users input:

- Commodity type
- Quantity available
- Location
- Certifications
- Packaging capability
- Logistics readiness

System outputs:

- Export Readiness Score (0–100)
- Required actions to qualify
- Risk indicators

---

## 4. Supply Listing (Producer Module)

Producers can:

- List available produce
- Upload quantity & harvest timelines
- Define supply windows
- Attach quality notes/images

Exporters can:

- Discover supply
- Filter by readiness, location, volume

---

## 5. Demand Requests (Buyer Module)

Buyers can:

- Submit sourcing requests
- Define quantity, destination, timeline
- Receive matched exporters

---

## 6. Smart Matching (Assisted — Not Fully Automated)

System suggests:

- Matching producers to exporters
- Matching exporters to buyer demand

Human approval remains in loop (important for MVP trust).

---

## 7. Workflow-Based Communication

- Built-in messaging between parties
- Inquiry tracking
- AI-assisted reply drafting
- Status-based deal progression

---

## 8. Export Planning Workspace

Each trade opportunity includes:

- Timeline (Gantt support)
- Task tracking
- Documentation checklist
- Milestone updates

---

## 9. Analytics & Insights

Dashboards include:

- Commodity performance
- Inquiry conversion rates
- Export cycle timelines
- Supply reliability tracking

---

## 🟡 Stage 2 — Operational SaaS (Revenue-Driving System)

### 🎯 Goal

Turn Agrihype into daily operational software for exporters.

---

## 10. SaaS Subscription System

- Tiered pricing plans
- Feature gating by subscription
- Usage-based limits
- Organization billing controls

---

## 11. Inventory Intelligence Layer

- Live stock management
- Harvest forecasting
- Supply reliability scoring
- Export capacity projections

---

## 12. Documentation Assistant (AI-Augmented)

System helps generate:

- Export preparation checklists
- Compliance guidance (per destination)
- Shipment readiness summaries

---

## 13. Deal Rooms (Transaction Workspaces)

Each transaction includes:

- Messages
- Documents
- Status tracking
- Assigned participants
- Activity history

---

## 14. Automation Triggers (Early “n8n-like” Layer)

Examples:

- Buyer request → notify matching exporter
- Stock upload → trigger demand scan
- Readiness passed → suggest export plan

---

## 🔵 Stage 3 — AI-Augmented Trade Network

### 🎯 Goal

Make Agrihype an intelligence engine for export coordination.

---

## 15. Predictive Export Recommendations

AI forecasts:

- Demand trends
- Price movement
- Export timing optimization
- Destination prioritization

---

## 16. Trust & Reputation System

Participants earn:

- Reliability scores
- Fulfillment history
- Quality consistency index

---

## 17. Autonomous Workflow Agents

Domain-specific automation agents that:

- Respond to buyer inquiries
- Check stock availability automatically
- Recommend fulfillment options
- Escalate to humans when needed

---

## 18. Advanced Matching Engine

AI recommends:

- Best exporter for each order
- Best producer for supply continuity
- Risk-adjusted trade pairings

---

## 🔴 Stage 4 — Enterprise Infrastructure Platform

### 🎯 Goal

Position Agrihype as trade infrastructure, not just a SaaS tool.

---

## 19. Multi-Market Compliance Intelligence

- Destination-specific export validation
- Regulatory readiness insights
- Risk alerts

---

## 20. Supply Chain Visibility Layer

- Commodity lifecycle tracking
- Fulfillment analytics
- Delay prediction models

---

## 21. Institutional Dashboards

For:

- Large exporters
- Cooperatives
- Trade organizations

Includes:

- Regional export intelligence
- Supply resilience metrics
- Commodity performance analysis

---

## 22. API & Ecosystem Integrations

Expose APIs for:

- Logistics providers
- Inspection agencies
- Trade finance partners
- External ERPs

---

# 🚀 Product Positioning

**Agrihype is not a marketplace.  
It is an intelligence and coordination layer between agricultural supply and global demand.**

---

# ✅ MVP Build Priority (What to Build First)

1. Multi-role authentication & organizations
2. Export intelligence dashboard
3. Readiness scoring system
4. Supply & demand listings
5. Matching suggestions
6. Communication workflows
7. Export planning workspace
8. Core analytics

---

# ❌ Defer Until Later

- Voice AI
- Deep automation builders
- Custom ML training
- Heavy logistics integrations
- Over-engineered AI

---

# 📈 Success Metric for MVP

You are successful when:

- Exporters actively use dashboards weekly
- Deals are initiated through the platform
- Real trade coordination happens inside Agrihype
- Users are willing to pay subscription fees
